Reading materials for Introduction to AI for Financial Applications

Machine Learning in Finance: deconstructing published papers (Barbara Będowska-Sójka)

Paper cards (one slide per paper): Day 4 - Machine Learning in Finance/Paper_cards.pdf. Suggested pre-reading: Mullainathan & Spiess (2017); Gu, Kelly & Xiu (2020), Sections 1–2.1 and Table 1.

URL: https://doi.org/10.1257/jep.31.2.87
